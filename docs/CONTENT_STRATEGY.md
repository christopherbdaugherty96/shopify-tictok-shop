# Content Strategy

The first goal of content is attention and testing.

Do not overcomplicate content at the beginning.

## Main Platforms

Start with:

- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook page/profile

Optional later:

- Pinterest
- YouTube Shorts

## Content Types

### Product Mockups

Simple shirt images showing:
- front view
- lifestyle view
- close-up detail

### Short Videos

Examples:
- shirt reveal
- mockup slideshow
- design process
- "would you wear this?"
- niche humor content
- trend audio with shirt showcase

### Meme-Style Content

Use humor or relatability connected to the niche.

## Posting Goal

The first goal is not perfection.

The first goal is:
- attention
- feedback
- comments
- shares
- clicks
- identifying winning designs

## Posting Frequency

Starting target:

- 1 to 3 posts per day if possible

Consistency matters more than perfection.

## Content Tracking

Track posts in:

`trackers/content_calendar.csv`

Track:
- date
- platform
- post idea
- product
- views
- likes
- comments
- shares
- clicks
- notes

## Do Not Focus On Early

Avoid spending too much time on:
- cinematic videos
- expensive equipment
- perfect branding
- paid ads
- overediting

The first goal is learning what people react to.
