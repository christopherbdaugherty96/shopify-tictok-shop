# Legal and Tax Notes

This document is a starting checklist, not legal or tax advice.

## Important Starting Concepts

You are running a real business once you begin selling products.

That means:
- income may be taxable
- payment processors may request identity verification
- platforms may request tax information
- customers may request refunds or support

## Avoid Copyright Problems

Do not use:
- sports team logos
- celebrity images
- movie/show characters
- trademarked slogans
- copyrighted artwork
- copied Etsy/Amazon designs

Create original designs.

## Business Structure

Starting path:

You can usually start as a sole proprietor initially.

Later you may decide to:
- form an LLC
- separate business banking
- register a formal business
- obtain accounting support

## Payment and Banking

Recommended:

- separate business income from personal spending as early as possible
- track revenue and costs
- keep order records
- keep payout records

## Sales Tracking

Track:
- revenue
- product costs
- refunds
- platform fees
- shipping costs
- ad spending later

Use:

`trackers/sales_pipeline.csv`

## Taxes

You may eventually need:
- tax filing
- sales tax handling
- bookkeeping
- income tracking

Do not ignore taxes once sales begin.

## Refunds and Customer Support

Create clear:
- shipping policy
- refund policy
- contact method

Handle customers professionally even when the business is small.
