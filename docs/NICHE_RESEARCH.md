# Niche Research

The first business decision is choosing an audience.

## Why Niches Matter

General shirts for everyone are difficult to grow.

Niche products are easier because:
- the audience already exists
- content becomes easier
- designs become easier
- humor becomes easier
- targeting becomes easier

## Good Starting Niches

Examples:

- gym humor
- dog owners
- blue collar trades
- gaming
- car culture
- faith-based
- funny relationships
- local pride
- fishing
- parenting humor
- coffee culture
- outdoor culture

## How To Research

Look at:

- TikTok comments
- Instagram pages
- Etsy best sellers
- Amazon merch trends
- Reddit communities
- Pinterest trends

## What To Look For

Find:
- repeated jokes
- repeated phrases
- strong identity
- emotional reactions
- people tagging friends
- comments saying “I need this”

## Avoid At First

Avoid:
- extremely broad audiences
- copyrighted content
- highly saturated copycat designs
- trying to target everyone

## First Goal

The first goal is not building a giant brand.

The first goal is finding a design style or niche people react to.
