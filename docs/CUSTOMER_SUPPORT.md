# Customer Support

Customer support is part of the business even when fulfillment is handled by Printify or Printful.

## Support Responsibilities

You are responsible for:
- responding to customer questions
- explaining shipping timelines
- helping with sizing questions
- handling damaged or wrong item reports
- communicating professionally

The fulfillment provider handles:
- printing
- packing
- shipping
- tracking updates

## Support Channels

Start simple:
- business email
- Shopify contact form
- social media DMs if necessary

Recommended support email format:

```txt
support@brandname.com
```

or early-stage:

```txt
brandname@gmail.com
```

## Common Customer Questions

### Where is my order?

Check Shopify order tracking first.

Reply with:

```txt
Thanks for reaching out. Your order is currently being processed/shipped. Here is the tracking information: [tracking link].
```

### What size should I order?

Use the product size chart from the fulfillment provider.

Do not guess sizing if you are unsure.

### My item arrived damaged.

Ask for:
- order number
- photo of damage
- photo of packaging if relevant

Then check the Printify/Printful support process.

### I entered the wrong address.

If the order has not gone into production, update it if possible.

If it has already shipped, explain that changes may no longer be possible.

## Support Rules

- Reply calmly.
- Keep records.
- Do not argue with customers.
- Do not promise something the provider cannot do.
- Check the actual order status before answering.
- Review the first orders manually.
