# Order Flow

This document explains how an order moves through the business.

## Customer Order Flow

1. Customer sees content.
2. Customer visits Shopify store.
3. Customer places order.
4. Shopify processes payment.
5. Printify or Printful receives fulfillment request.
6. Provider prints shirt.
7. Provider ships shirt.
8. Tracking information updates.
9. Customer receives order.
10. You handle support if needed.

## Your Responsibility

You are responsible for:
- design quality
- listing accuracy
- customer communication
- pricing
- brand presentation
- reviewing orders
- handling problems

## Fulfillment Provider Responsibility

The provider handles:
- printing
- packaging
- shipping
- fulfillment updates

## Important Rule

Review the first several orders carefully.

Do not assume everything is working automatically.

Verify:
- product quality
- shipping times
- tracking updates
- customer emails
- sizing information
- product photos
