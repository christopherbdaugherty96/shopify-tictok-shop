# How To Identify Winning Products

The first goal is finding products people react to.

A winning product does not always mean massive sales immediately.

## Early Winning Signals

Look for:
- high views
- shares
- comments
- people tagging friends
- profile clicks
- product clicks
- repeat engagement
- comments saying "I need this"

## Strong Indicators

Good signs:
- people understand the design quickly
- the niche reacts emotionally
- viewers relate to the joke/message
- content performs repeatedly

## Weak Product Signals

Weak signs:
- no engagement
- confusing message
- unreadable design
- poor mockups
- weak niche targeting

## Important Rule

Do not judge products too quickly.

Test:
- multiple posts
- multiple hooks
- different mockups
- different captions

before assuming a product failed.

## Winning Product Workflow

1. Create concept.
2. Create design.
3. Create mockups.
4. Post content.
5. Track engagement.
6. Improve strong concepts.
7. Remove weak concepts.

## Scaling A Strong Product

If a design performs well:
- create variations
- create more content
- improve listing quality
- test additional products
- improve branding around the niche

## Important Rule

The goal is not random products.

The goal is finding repeatable audience reactions.
