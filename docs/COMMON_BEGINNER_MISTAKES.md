# Common Beginner Mistakes

## Mistake 1: Starting Too Big

Do not start with:
- 50 designs
- multiple niches
- complicated branding
- paid ads immediately
- multiple storefronts

Start small.

## Mistake 2: Waiting For Perfection

Your first products and content will improve over time.

Launch anyway.

## Mistake 3: Copying Copyrighted Content

Avoid:
- sports teams
- celebrities
- movie/show references
- trademarked phrases
- copied Etsy designs

This can create legal and platform problems.

## Mistake 4: Ignoring Mockup Quality

Bad mockups hurt trust.

Use:
- clean images
- readable designs
- realistic shirt previews

## Mistake 5: Overcomplicated Automation

Do not start with:
- AI agents
- autonomous posting
- complex integrations
- expensive software stacks

First prove the business manually.

## Mistake 6: Not Posting Consistently

One post is not enough.

Consistency matters.

## Mistake 7: Not Tracking Results

Track:
- views
- clicks
- comments
- sales
- winning designs

Otherwise you will not know what is working.

## Mistake 8: Pricing Too Low

Cheap pricing can destroy margins.

Remember:
- product costs
- shipping
- platform fees
- refunds
- future ad costs

## Mistake 9: Trying To Target Everyone

Strong niches perform better.

## Mistake 10: Giving Up Too Early

The first phase is testing and learning.

Do not expect instant success from the first few posts or products.
