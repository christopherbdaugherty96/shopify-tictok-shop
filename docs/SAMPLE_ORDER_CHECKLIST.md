# Sample Order Checklist

Before heavily promoting products, place a sample order if possible.

## Goal

Verify:
- print quality
- shirt quality
- shipping process
- packaging
- tracking updates
- customer emails
- overall experience

## Checklist

### Product Quality

- Is the print centered correctly?
- Are colors accurate?
- Is the image blurry?
- Does the shirt material feel acceptable?
- Does sizing match expectations?

### Mockup Accuracy

- Does the real shirt resemble the mockup?
- Was anything misleading?

### Shipping

- Did tracking work correctly?
- Was shipping time reasonable?
- Did updates arrive properly?

### Customer Experience

- Were confirmation emails clear?
- Was checkout smooth?
- Was the product page accurate?

## Important Rule

Do not rely entirely on mockups.

Seeing the real product matters.
