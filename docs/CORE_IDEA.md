# Core Idea

This repo is a manual-first ecommerce workflow system for creating and selling products online.

It supports:
- design creation
- T-shirt/product planning
- Shopify listing drafts
- TikTok/Instagram post drafts
- ad copy drafts
- sales tracking
- product improvement loops

The human remains the operator.

AI may assist with drafting, analysis, design prompts, captions, and organization, but publishing, posting, customer contact, and sales decisions remain manually reviewed.
