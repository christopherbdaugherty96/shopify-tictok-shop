# Shipping and Returns

This document explains the basic shipping and return process.

## Shipping Flow

1. Customer places order.
2. Shopify processes payment.
3. Printify or Printful receives order.
4. Product enters production.
5. Product ships.
6. Tracking updates become available.

## Important Rule

Production time is separate from shipping time.

Customers may confuse the two.

## Shipping Expectations

Do not promise unrealistic shipping times.

Always check:
- provider production estimates
- shipping estimates
- holiday delays
- high-demand periods

## Tracking

Customers should receive:
- shipping updates
- tracking number when available

Always verify tracking before responding to support questions.

## Returns

Print-on-demand products are usually handled differently from mass inventory stores.

Create clear policies for:
- damaged items
- wrong items
- lost packages
- incorrect addresses

## Recommended Policy Style

Keep policies:
- clear
- simple
- realistic
- visible on the storefront

## Important Rule

Do not create fake guarantees or unrealistic promises.

Be honest about production and shipping timelines.
