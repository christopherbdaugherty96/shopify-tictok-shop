# How To Price Products

Pricing is important because profit margins in print-on-demand can become small quickly.

## Basic Formula

```txt
Retail Price
- Product Cost
- Shipping
- Platform Fees
= Estimated Profit
```

## Example

```txt
Retail Price: $24.99
Product Cost: $10.00
Shipping Estimate: $5.00
Platform Fees Estimate: $2.00
Estimated Profit: $7.99
```

## Important Concepts

Do not forget:
- shipping
- taxes
- refunds
- payment processing fees
- discounts
- future advertising costs

## Beginner Pricing Mistake

Many beginners price too low.

Low prices can:
- reduce profit heavily
- make scaling difficult
- create refund problems
- make ads impossible later

## Compare Similar Products

Research:
- Etsy
- Shopify brands
- Amazon merch
- TikTok Shop products

Do not blindly copy pricing.

## Test Pricing

You may eventually test:
- different prices
- bundles
- limited offers
- free shipping thresholds

## Important Rule

A product getting attention is more important initially than perfect pricing.

But the business must still make profit.
