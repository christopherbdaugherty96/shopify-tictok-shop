# Payment and Fulfillment

This document explains how money and fulfillment move through the business.

## Business Flow

1. Customer buys through Shopify.
2. Shopify processes payment.
3. The order is sent to Printify or Printful.
4. The provider prints the shirt.
5. The provider ships the shirt.
6. Shopify updates order tracking.
7. You monitor the order and customer communication.

## Important Concept

You are not buying inventory first.

This is print-on-demand.

The shirt is created after the customer purchases it.

## Shopify Payments Setup

Recommended starting setup:

- Shopify Payments
- PayPal later if desired

You will likely need:
- legal name
- address
- bank account
- payout information
- identity verification

## Bank Account

Recommended:

Use a separate account for business income as early as possible.

This makes:
- taxes easier
- tracking easier
- profit visibility easier

## Product Pricing

Basic pricing structure:

```txt
Retail Price
- Product Cost
- Shipping
- Platform Fees
= Estimated Profit
```

Do not forget fees and shipping.

## First Test Order

Before advertising heavily:

1. Place a test order if possible.
2. Verify checkout works.
3. Verify payment works.
4. Verify shipping updates work.
5. Verify customer emails work.
6. Verify product quality.

## Refunds

Create a clear refund policy.

At minimum, define:
- damaged item process
- wrong item process
- shipping delays
- contact method

## Customer Communication

Customers should be able to:
- contact you
- ask about shipping
- ask about sizes
- ask about problems

Do not ignore customer communication.

## Important Rule

Do not assume automation is perfect.

Review:
- first orders
- tracking updates
- payment payouts
- customer emails
- mockup accuracy
- print quality
