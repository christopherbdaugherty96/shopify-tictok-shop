## Brand Voice

The brand should sound:

- simple
- direct
- relatable
- not corporate
- not overcomplicated

## Visual Style

Starting style:

- readable text designs
- simple graphics
- strong contrast
- mockups that clearly show the shirt

Avoid:

- copyrighted logos
- celebrity names/images
- sports team references
- trademarked phrases
- copied designs

## First Brand Rule

The brand does not need to be perfect before launch.

The first goal is to test whether people respond to the designs.
