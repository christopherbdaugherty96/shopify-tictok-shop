# Auralis Art and Apparel Model

Auralis can include both clothing products and occasional standalone artwork.

## Core Direction

Auralis is an art-led brand built around:

- tie-dye inspired visuals
- geometric designs
- abstract paintings
- personal artwork
- wearable art
- niche-tailored collections
- occasional standalone art pieces

The brand should not feel like a generic T-shirt shop.

It should feel like a visual world where artwork can become clothing, prints, or limited drops.

## Product Categories

### 1. Apparel

Primary starting products:
- T-shirts
- hoodies later
- sweatshirts later

These are fulfilled through print-on-demand when possible.

### 2. Art Prints

Occasional artwork can be listed as:
- digital art showcase
- wall prints
- poster-style products
- limited visual drops

These should support the brand identity, not distract from it.

### 3. Personal Works

Personal abstract paintings can become:
- product artwork
- collection visuals
- landing page visuals
- limited edition designs
- art story content


## Checkout Strategy

Do not build custom checkout first.

Recommended flow:

```txt
Auralis website
→ product or collection page
→ buy link
→ Shopify product page or checkout
→ Printify/Printful fulfillment
```

## Art Drop Rule

Artwork should be added intentionally.

Do not turn the website into a random gallery.

Every art piece should connect to:
- the visual identity
- a collection
- a product idea
- a story
- a future apparel design

## AI Role

AI may help with:
- turning artwork into apparel concepts
- creating niche-tailored product directions
- writing collection descriptions
- generating mockup ideas
- naming drops
- drafting social posts

Human judgment decides what belongs in the brand.

## First Version

Start with:
- 3 collections
- 3 to 9 apparel products
- 1 small artwork/personal works section
- simple buy links
- manual posting
- Shopify/POD backend

The goal is to test whether people respond to the art style and product direction.
