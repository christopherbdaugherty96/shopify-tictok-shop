# Nova Later

Nova is not part of the first operating version of this repo.

This repo is about starting and operating the business manually first.

First, the business process must exist:
- product ideas
- designs
- mockups
- Shopify listings
- TikTok and Instagram content
- manual posting
- sales tracking
- customer process
- improvement loop

Later, Nova may assist with:
- organizing product ideas
- reading trackers
- drafting listing copy
- drafting social posts
- summarizing sales patterns
- identifying missing workflow steps

Nova should not initially:
- publish Shopify products
- post to TikTok or Instagram
- message customers
- spend ad money
- change prices
- run hidden background automation
